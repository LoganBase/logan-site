Market Hub — User & Architecture Guide

An Institutional-Grade Market Assessment Support Document

1. Executive Overview & Architecture

The Market Hub is an institutional-grade financial decision-support dashboard designed to provide a systematic, data-driven framework for assessing market regimes, rally quality, participation breadth, historical valuations, yield structures, and capital flows. The platform translates complex multi-asset indicators into actionable risk parameters, helping investors distinguish between healthy, broad-based expansions and fragile, narrow market environments.

At the core of the Market Hub is its Card-based Architecture. The interface is organized into 10 active diagnostic panels (with 1 additional staging panel) categorized into three structural pillars: Trend / Momentum, Participation, and Macro Conditions. By systematically navigating through these cards, an investor can transition from high-level market direction checks down to granular asset class flows and execution levels. This workflow prevents emotional decision-making by enforcing strict quantitative thresholds and logical overrides across all major asset classes.

2. Card-by-Card Framework

Card 01 — Regime ("The Anchor")

High-Level Purpose: Identifies the primary trend of the S&P 500 (SPY) and assesses whether the prevailing market environment is structurally bullish, extended, or in a secular bear market.

KPI Metric / Row Indicator Definition Banding Thresholds & Interpretation
SPY Regime SPY Close vs. 200-day Simple Moving Average (SMA) Bullish: SPY > 200d SMA ("Secular Bull — Stay Long")
Bearish: SPY < 200d SMA ("Secular Bear — Reduce Exposure")
Stretch Risk Percentage distance of SPY price from its 200d SMA Bearish (Overextended): > +14%
Neutral (Extended): > +10% to +14%
Bullish (Normal Bull): 0% to +10%
Neutral (Bearish Retest): -10% to 0%
Bearish (Oversold): < -10%
Trend Cross The percentage spread between the 50d SMA and the 200d SMA Bullish: > +8% ("Golden Cross — Confirmed")
Neutral: -8% to +8% ("Cross Forming — Awaiting Confirmation")
Bearish: < -8% ("Death Cross — De-Risk")
Override Rule: If SPY falls below its 200d SMA (Row 0 is Bearish), the entire Card 1 status is overridden to bearish, suppressing any bullish signals from other rows and displaying the global regimeBearish banner.

3. Deep-Dive Section Analysis: Card 01 — Regime

The Regime panel serves as the core tactical and strategic filter for the entire dashboard. When an investor expands Card 01, they access the primary execution engine. It combines real-time SPY price metrics with long-term trend lines, historical percentile evaluations, and trend velocity indicators.

[INSERT SCREENSHOT: Card 01 expanded, showcasing the DeepChartLg SVG chart with SPY price and 50d/200d SMA overlays, and the timeline below it]

Chart Interpretations

The primary SVG line chart (DeepChartLg) displays the absolute price of the S&P 500 Index (SPY) as its primary series, rendered in cyan (#22d3ee). It overlays two key moving averages: the 50-day SMA and the 200-day SMA. The relative positioning of these three lines helps identify three main market stages:

  • Price above 50d above 200d: An established, healthy uptrend. Pullbacks to the 50-day or 200-day moving averages represent potential low-risk entry points.
  • Price below 50d but above 200d: A short-term corrective consolidation within a secular bull market. This phase is characterized by elevated volatility but does not require liquidation of core long holdings.
  • Price below 200d: A secular bear market. High-multiple growth positions should be reduced, hedge allocations increased, and cash levels raised.

Regime History

The color-coded historical timeline provides a long-term view of market health. It displays visual transitions across three primary market regimes:

  • Secular Bull (Green): Active when SPY is trading above its 200d SMA and the 50d/200d spread is constructive. Investors should carry full risk exposure.
  • Overextended Bull (Amber/Yellow): Triggered when SPY extends > 10% to 14% above its 200d SMA. This warns of exhaustion and elevated correction risk, suggesting a halt to new capital allocations.
  • Secular Bear (Red): Activated immediately when SPY falls below the 200d SMA. Tactical equity exposure should be reduced.

Regime Metrics Deep-Dive

The Regime card renders three specialized statistical boxes to evaluate the maturity of the prevailing trend:

  1. Percentile Rank: Measures where the current distance from the 200d SMA sits relative to all historical daily readings since 2006. A reading ≥ 90th percentile signals extreme extension, while a reading ≤ 10th percentile highlights deep capitulation.
  2. Regime Duration: Tracks the exact number of consecutive trading days the S&P 500 has spent above or below its 200d SMA. A duration > 250 days suggests a mature trend, while a duration < 10 days indicates a newly formed, unconfirmed breakout.
  3. Extension Velocity: Measures the 10-day Rate of Change of the stretch risk percentage. This metric shows whether the trend is accelerating (positive velocity) or slowing down (negative velocity).

Core Analytical Questions An investor Can Answer

  • Is the current S&P 500 price extension statistically sustainable, or is the index approaching historical stretch extremes?
  • How mature is the prevailing regime, and is the trend acceleration (velocity) showing signs of exhaustion?
  • Are the short-term trend (50d SMA) and long-term trend (200d SMA) aligned, or is a Golden/Death cross currently forming?
  • Does the absolute SPY position relative to its 200-day moving average require a defensive portfolio posture?
  • Is the market showing a constructive retest of support, or is it breaking down into a secular bear market?

5. Support & Appendices

For assistance with data delays, API connections, or interpreting indicators, navigate to the Support tab at the top-right of the dashboard interface. You can access developer logs, run system diagnostic reports, or submit tickets here.

Troubleshooting Index:

  • Indicator showing "Awaiting Data" (—): Occurs when the database (Cloudflare D1) has not seeded. The platform will try to fall back to Yahoo Finance. If the issue continues, contact system administration.
  • Stale Data Warning: If the database is stale by more than one business day, the chart status indicator will turn amber and show Stale · YYYY-MM-DD. Check the database migration scripts.
  • Cache Inconsistencies: If UI elements appear misaligned after an update, clear your browser cache or verify the version parameter is bumped in your HTML configuration file.
