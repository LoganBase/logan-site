1. Webcast Transcription Summary

Title: Breadth Broadens as Inflation Heats Back Up
Publisher: Barometer Capital Management
Speaker: David Burrows, Chief Investment Officer (CIO)
Air Date: June 17, 2026

Core Themes Transcribed:

  • Widespread Market Participation: Burrows argues that market breadth is in an expansion phase. Market leadership is broadening beyond a few mega-cap technology companies, showing solid strength in both Canadian and broader international equity indexes.
  • The Inflation Second Wave: Economic indicators (CPI, PPI, and import/export price metrics) are consistently reporting higher than expected. Barometer believes a structural "second wave" of inflation is forming, which will keep central bank rates higher for longer.
  • Sectors with Pricing Power: Due to inflation, Burrows recommends allocating to groups with structural pricing power. Specifically, he highlights **Financials, Industrials, Materials, Energy, and Semiconductors**. Conversely, he advises avoiding interest-rate-sensitive "bond-like" defensive sectors (such as utilities and consumer staples), which are lagging.
  • Portfolio Protection & Cash: While structural trends are positive, Barometer is maintaining approximately 9% cash. This buffer is positioned to navigate typical seasonal summer volatility and political uncertainty ahead of the US elections.
  • Precious Metals Recovery: Burrows comments that the recent correction in Gold and Silver has concluded, clearing out weaker speculative positions and establishing a base for another upward leg.

3. In-Depth Alignment Analysis

Key Alignment: Global Breadth Expansion
The most significant alignment is in global equity participation. David Burrows' claim that market breadth is expanding matches the Market Hub's Global Flows (Card 8), where all 7 major regional indices (USA, Europe, Asia, LatAm, Canada, Emerging, and Global) are trading above their 200d SMAs. Similarly, the dashboard's Sector Check (Card 3, Row 2) shows 9 out of 11 sectors trading in uptrends, supporting the "broad participation" view.
Tactical Divergence: Sector Leadership Spreads
A short-term conflict exists on sector rotation. Burrows states that cyclicals (Financials, Industrials) are leading while defensives are lagging. However, the Market Hub's Sectors (Card 9) shows a bearish status with a cyclical-to-defensive relative spread of -2.8%. This is because the dashboard's sector status is driven by a fast 20-day relative return momentum window, which is picking up a short-term defensive flight, whereas Barometer is looking at a longer-term structural trend.
Structural Divergence: Commodity Performance
The most critical conflict is in real assets. Barometer is structurally bullish on commodities (Energy, Materials, Gold) as inflation hedges. Meanwhile, the Market Hub's Commodities (Card 10) is bearish, showing that broad commodities (USCI) and metals are trading below their 200-day moving averages. The scoring engine requires real assets to reclaim these trend lines before validating the inflation-hedge thesis.

4. Recommendations for Dashboard Enhancements

  1. Implement Multi-Timeframe Sector Momentum: To resolve the conflict on sector rotation, Card 09 (Sectors) should display both a **Tactical (20-day)** relative return and a **Strategic (200-day)** relative return to capture both short-term rotation and structural leadership trends.
  2. Track Inflation Metrics Directly: To capture the "second wave" of inflation discussed in the webcast, add an inflation tracking row (such as Core CPI YoY or PPI) to Card 05 (Yield) or Card 04 (Valuations).
  3. Refine Precious Metals Banding: The commodities card should incorporate a spread tracking row (e.g. Copper vs. Gold ratio) to quantitatively capture when industrial growth is leading safe-haven demand.
